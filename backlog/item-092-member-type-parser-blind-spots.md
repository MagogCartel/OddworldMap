# 92. The member-type parser's remaining blind spots

**Status:** open — the sub-struct arm remains; the `class`-declared struct and the uppercase member closed 2026-09-19 · **Effort:** small to medium (builder), no disc · **Where:** anywhere with the checkout · **Filed:** 2026-09-01

## What it is

`parse_member_types` types a field by finding its declaration in a data struct's body. Three declaration shapes escaped it, and the sub-struct arm is the one still open, so nine placed objects still render `scale` raw and a few real enums ride with them:

- **A sub-struct arm.** Nine scales sit inside a `_data`/`_Data` struct whose owning member the `_data` filter drops while the CTOR reads one arm of it through a dot (`ADD("Scale", mTlv.mData.scale)` on the AO stones, `mTlv.field_10_data.field_1C_scale` on Teleporter): AO `BellSongStone`, `HandStone`, `MovieStone`; AE `Drill`, `FlyingSlig`, `FlyingSligSpawner`, `Slurg`, `SlurgSpawner`, `Teleporter`. `parse_object_schema` collapses the ADD's expression to its first segment, so the arm's own declared type is never consulted — for most of the nine it already sits in the member table (`Path_Teleporter_Data`, `Path_Drill_Data` and the stones' data structs are all swept), while Slurg's `Slurg_Path_Data` is not `Path_`-prefixed and needs the struct scan widened as well. Resolving the arm also types Drill's `behavior` (`DrillBehavior`), Slurg's `start_direction` (`XDirection_short`), Teleporter's `level` (`LevelIds`) and `cam_swap_effect` (`ScreenChangeEffects`), and retires the Drill `start_direction` override, whose "outside the Tlvs include graph" reason never held — Drill.hpp is included and `Path_Drill_Data` sweeps fine. An interim override would label `behavior` the way `start_direction` is labelled today, at the cost of a second entry this fix has to retire. It is the same mechanism that would resolve the union arms [41](item-041-inherited-field-types.md) left unresolved: AO WellExpress's `off_level` is `LevelIds` behind the union's `.level` arm.
- **A `class`-declared data struct.** `Path_MovingBomb` is the one `class Path_*` in either game's swept headers, so its body is never read: its `Scale_short` scale and two `Choice_short` flags (`triggered_by_alarm`, `persist_offscreen`) ship bare, and MovingBomb is absent from `field_types_ao.json` entirely. The fix is one token — `\b(?:struct|class)\s+` — plus a cache regen; the access-specifier line carries no semicolon, so the member regex already skips it.
- **An uppercase member.** `SligGetPants` declares `Scale_short Scale;` and the member regex admits lowercase-initial names only. Widening it needs a sweep of what else an uppercase-initial match would admit before it ships.

## Why it matters

[29](item-029-decomp-label-sweep.md)'s missing-enum scan read the schema cache, which is blind to whatever this parser misses, and listed these as upstream misdeclarations — none is: every one declares its type somewhere the parser cannot see. Until the arm is resolved, nine objects show `scale` as a bare 0/1 and the named enums render raw.

## Shipped

**The `class`-declared data struct and the uppercase member, 2026-09-19.** Two tokens, as the bullets sketched: `\b(?:struct|class)\s+` in the struct scan and `[A-Za-z_]\w*` in the member regex, then an object-cache regen and a sidecar re-emit, with no disc in it — the third element is metadata and `map_data` never carried it. The sweep the uppercase bullet asked for came back at exactly one member across both games, `Path_SligGetPants::Scale`, and the `class` token at one struct, whose three `PSX_Point` rect members are the inherited noise all 97 of AO's other `Path_*` structs already carry. Four lines of sidecar followed: `MovingBomb`'s three typed fields in `field_types_ao.json`, `SligGetPants`'s `scale` in the AE file, and `enum_labels_*` byte-identical either way. AO's 35 moving bombs read `scale=full` and their two flags as `true`/`false` where they read 0 and 1, and AE's seven lockers `scale=full`; no marker moved, all 42 sitting on the foreground plane `onBackgroundPlane`'s untyped fallback already read them onto.

Only the struct scan took the token. The aggregate sweep and `parse_enum_labels`' enum-owner scan are still `struct`-only, and the one `class Path_*` declares neither a nested enum nor an aggregate-valued member, so widening those would have moved nothing — which is also why the enum labels came back unchanged.
